TINY 2.1

A transformer for DOS that fits on a floppy disk — running in your browser.

C:\TINY>_

Click or press Enter to start. Sound is on: PC speaker and robot voice.

Ask a question and press Enter: what is the capital of peru, is a whale a fish, what is 7 times 8, spell elephant, tell me a joke. It knows about 3,500 facts and makes things up confidently outside of them.

This is the original TINY.C, unchanged, compiled to WebAssembly. A small layer stands in for Turbo C's screen, keyboard and PC-speaker routines. The network is the original 380 KB TINY.BIN: 4 layers, 96 wide, 8-bit weights.

TINY 2.1 is by WollyMonjac (source, DOS executable and manual there).
